`pg-probe top` now has the look of the Kiso site: a dotted background, translucent panels and tabs with a rule under them. It uses more of the screen, with one column of margin on each side. The settings and log views show their side panel at full height, and the summary in the log view has a bold title. The help, explain and functions dialogs dim the screen behind them.
